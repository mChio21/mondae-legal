The service

Mondae is a text-message business assistant operated by Michael Chiovitti (sole proprietor), Thomaston, Connecticut, USA ("Mondae", "we"). A business owner ("you", "the operator") texts Mondae to get the money side of the work done: estimates, invoices, receipts, payment records, reminders, and a short summary of where the business stands. Mondae drafts; you confirm; nothing goes to a customer unless you send it.

How it works with your customers

  • Every text to your customer comes from your own phone. Mondae writes the message and hands it to you with the estimate or invoice link; you choose the recipient from your own contacts and send it. Mondae's line never texts your customers.
  • Your customers accept estimates and pay invoices on pages Mondae hosts for your business. Each page carries your business name and your own phone number for questions.
  • You are responsible for the accuracy of your estimates, invoices, and records, for having your customers' permission to text them, and for your own dealings with them.

Texts from Mondae to you

  • Mondae texts you on its line: replies to what you ask, a Monday summary, a short Friday check-in, reminders when money is waiting, and notices when a customer accepts or pays. Message frequency varies with your use. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time and Mondae stops texting you; our messaging provider enforces this too. After STOP you receive one message with the link to cancel your account, and nothing more until you text Mondae again. STOP by itself does not cancel your subscription; see Canceling below.
  • For help, text Mondae "help" or email mike@sundaemedia.co.
  • Carriers and messaging providers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Price and billing

  • Mondae costs $199.99 a month, one price, everything included. Signing up is free. After your first estimate or invoice goes out, Mondae texts you a link to put a card on file; the first charge is made when you do, and the same amount is charged to that card every month on that date until you cancel.
  • Billing is handled by Stripe. Mondae never sees your card number. Stripe emails you a receipt for every charge, and Mondae records each charge as a software expense in your records.
  • Until a card is on file, Mondae answers your requests with the link instead of doing the work. The same happens if your card stops working for more than a week; updating the card restores everything.
  • A promotional code, when we offer one, is entered on the card page and applies as described there.

Canceling

  • Text Mondae "cancel my account" at any time. You receive a link to a page with three choices: keep your account as it is, pause it, or cancel. Canceling takes one tap on that page.
  • When you cancel, your account runs through the end of the month you have paid for, then stops. No partial-month refunds. Text "keep my account" before then and nothing changes.
  • If you need to cancel another way, email mike@sundaemedia.co from the email on your Stripe receipt and we will cancel it for you within two business days, effective the same way.

Pausing for the slow season

  • On the cancel page you can pause your account for one, two, or three months instead. Nothing is charged while paused, and your customers, jobs, and records stay in place.
  • The pause ends on the date shown when you choose it, and the $199.99 monthly charge resumes on that date without further notice; Mondae texts you the day it resumes. Text "I'm back" to end a pause early.
  • While paused, Mondae completes one request for you as a courtesy; further requests wait until you unpause.

Customer payments

Where you offer card or bank payment, the payment is processed by Stripe on your own Stripe account. You are the merchant; Mondae is not a party to the sale, never holds your money, and never stores card numbers. Stripe's fees come out of each payment; Mondae adds nothing. Cash and check payments you record in Mondae are records only; no money moves through Mondae.
