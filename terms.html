The service

Mondae is a text-message-based business assistant operated by Michael Chiovitti (sole proprietor), Thomaston, Connecticut, USA. A business owner ("the business") texts Mondae to get work done: estimates, invoices, receipts, payments, and a short summary of where the business stands. At the business's request, Mondae also sends messages to the business's own customers on the business's behalf, and passes their replies back.

How messaging works

  • Mondae sends and receives messages through a messaging provider. Depending on the recipient's device, a message may be delivered as iMessage, RCS, or SMS.
  • Mondae operates two kinds of conversations: one between the business and Mondae, and one between the business and its customers, which Mondae carries on the business's behalf.
  • Message frequency varies based on use of the service.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages from that conversation. Opt-outs are honored immediately and are also enforced by our messaging provider.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email mike@sundaemedia.co.
  • Carriers and messaging providers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Business owners

A business joins Mondae by texting the Mondae line first ([MONDAE LINE]). Only people who text the line first receive messages from it. Everything Mondae sends to a business's customers is drafted at the business's request and approved by the business before it goes out. The business is responsible for the accuracy of its estimates, invoices, and other business records, and for its own dealings with its customers.

Customers of a business

If you are a customer of a business that uses Mondae, the messages you receive come from that business, through Mondae, and every message identifies the business. Mondae contacts you only after you have texted the business's line, or when the business asks Mondae to send you a document for a job you gave the business your number for. Reply STOP at any time and you will receive nothing further from that business through Mondae. Your replies go to the business.

Payments

Where a business offers card payment through Mondae, the payment is processed by Stripe on the business's own Stripe account. The business is the merchant; Mondae is not a party to the sale and never stores card numbers. Cash and check payments that a business records in Mondae are records only; no money moves through Mondae.

Acceptable use

The service may not be used for unlawful purposes, harassment, or unsolicited promotional messaging. Mondae does not send marketing messages to a business's customers. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms.

The service is provided "as is" during its beta period. Mondae records what it is told and summarizes it; it does not provide tax, legal, or accounting advice. Business documents prepared through the service are reviewed and approved by the business owner before sending.
