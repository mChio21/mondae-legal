Mondae — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE], replacing the September 1, 2026 version

Mondae ("we," "us") is a text-message-based business assistant operated by Michael Chiovitti (sole proprietor), Thomaston, Connecticut, USA. This policy describes how we handle information for businesses that use Mondae and for their customers.

Information we collect

From businesses: the owner's name, mobile phone number, the contents of messages sent to and from Mondae, photos texted to Mondae (such as receipts), and the business records created through the service at the owner's request: customers' names and phone numbers, estimates, invoices, payments, and receipts.

From a business's customers: the name and phone number the business gave us for a job, and the contents of messages exchanged with the business through Mondae. We collect nothing else about customers and we do not look them up elsewhere.

Mobile information and text messaging consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer mobile phone numbers or messaging opt-in consent to any third party for marketing. Opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How we use information

Information is used solely to provide the Mondae service: reading and responding to messages, preparing business documents, carrying messages between a business and its customers at the business's request, sending service-related notifications, recording payments and receipts, and producing summaries and reports for the business. Message contents are processed by an artificial-intelligence provider to understand requests and read receipt photos; that provider does not use Mondae's data to train its models.

Service providers

We use service providers only as needed to operate the service: a messaging provider that delivers messages over iMessage, RCS, and SMS (SOC 2 Type II certified; iMessage traffic is end-to-end encrypted in transit), an artificial-intelligence provider, a database and file-storage provider, a hosting provider, and, where a business offers card payment, Stripe, which processes payments on the business's own Stripe account. Card numbers are entered on Stripe's pages and never reach Mondae.

Text messaging disclosures

  • Message frequency varies based on use of the service.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Opt-outs are honored immediately.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at the email below.

Data retention and security

We keep our own copy of messages and business records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations; our messaging provider retains message contents only briefly to deliver them. Access to data is restricted, data is encrypted at rest by our providers, and passwords and credentials are never stored in plain text.

Your choices

A business can ask us to export or delete its data at any time. A customer of a business can reply STOP to stop messages, and can ask us to delete the information we hold about them. The service is not intended for anyone under 18.
