Mondae — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Mondae ("we," "us") is a text-message-based business assistant service operated by Michael Chiovitti (sole proprietor), Thomaston, Connecticut, USA. This policy describes how we handle information for the Mondae messaging program.

Information we collect

We collect the information participants provide directly: name, mobile phone number, and the contents of messages sent to and from the service, along with business records (such as estimates and invoices) created through the service at the user's request.

Mobile information and text messaging consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent to any third party for marketing. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How we use information

Information is used solely to provide the Mondae service: responding to messages, preparing business documents, sending service-related notifications, and improving the service. We use service providers (such as our messaging and hosting vendors) only as needed to operate the service.

Text messaging disclosures

  • Message frequency varies based on your use of the service.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Opt-outs are honored immediately.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at the email below.

Data retention and security

We retain records as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Access to data is restricted, and sensitive credentials are never stored in plain text.
